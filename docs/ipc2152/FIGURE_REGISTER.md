## Provenance requirements

When authorized source images or independently permitted numerical readings are added to a restricted validation repository, include: figure number, edition, page, origin, image hash, acquisition date, digitization method, digitization uncertainty, and person verifying extracted values.

Numerical models should cite the standard and clearly distinguish fit error from physical measurement uncertainty. Avoid committing scans or complete chart-derived point tables into the public repository until legal redistribution permission has been confirmed.
